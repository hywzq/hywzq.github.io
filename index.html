My work connects climate modelling, monsoon dynamics, vegetation–climate feedbacks, and paleoclimate data–model comparison. 我的研究连接气候模拟、季风动力学、植被—气候反馈与古气候数据—模式对比。

Academic Background学术背景

Zhenqian Wang is a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Copenhagen. He completed his Ph.D. in Earth System Science at Lanzhou University in 2023, including a year as a guest Ph.D. student in paleoclimate modelling at Stockholm University. He holds an M.Eng. in Environmental Engineering from Northwest Normal University and a B.Mgt. in Business Administration from Anhui Polytechnic University. 王振乾，哥本哈根大学博士后。2023 年于兰州大学获地球系统科学博士学位，其间赴斯德哥尔摩大学进行为期一年的气候模拟联合培养。此前获西北师范大学环境工程硕士学位、安徽工程大学工商管理学士学位。

He develops and tunes the EC-Earth Earth system model for paleoclimate applications, and has contributed coupled climate–vegetation simulations to the CMIP6–PMIP4 archive. 主要从事 EC-Earth 地球系统模式的古气候应用开发与性能调优，并向 CMIP6–PMIP4 数据集贡献了多组气候—植被耦合模拟结果。

Research Focus研究重点

Research interests include paleoclimate modelling, East Asian summer monsoon dynamics, vegetation–climate feedbacks, orbital forcing across interglacial periods, global monsoon projections, and ecosystem water–carbon responses. 研究方向包括古气候模拟、东亚夏季风动力学、植被—气候反馈、间冰期轨道强迫、全球季风预估，以及生态系统水碳响应。
