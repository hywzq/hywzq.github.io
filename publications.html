Zhenqian Wang’s research takes climate modelling as its starting point, anchored by three core foci: monsoon dynamics, vegetation–climate feedbacks, and Earth system model development. Through this framework, the work explores how orbital forcing and vegetation change jointly shape monsoon precipitation and climate variability across glacial–interglacial cycles. 王振乾的研究以气候模拟为起点，围绕三个核心方向展开：季风动力学、植被—气候反馈与地球系统模式开发。通过这一框架，探讨轨道强迫与植被变化如何共同塑造季风降水与冰期—间冰期循环中的气候变率。

As a first or corresponding author, Zhenqian Wang has published 8 papers in journals including Scientific Reports, Advances in Atmospheric Sciences, Quaternary Sciences, Ecological Indicators and Paleoceanography and Paleoclimatology. In total, 54 journal papers span 2018 to 2026, of which 12 are currently under review or in revision. 作为第一作者或通讯作者，王振乾已在 Scientific Reports、Advances in Atmospheric Sciences、第四纪研究、Ecological Indicators、Paleoceanography and Paleoclimatology 等期刊发表论文 8 篇。累计发表期刊论文 54 篇，时间跨度 2018–2026 年，其中 12 篇处于审稿或修改阶段。
