Monsoon Dynamics and Variability季风动力学与变率

Northward migration of the East Asian summer monsoon northern boundary during the 21st century

Wang Z., Fu Z., Liu B., Zheng Z., Zhang W., Liu Y., Zhang F., Zhang Q.

Scientific Reports, 2022, 12: 10066.

The northern boundary of the East Asian summer monsoon is defined here from combined precipitation and circulation criteria rather than precipitation alone. Tracking that boundary through 21st-century simulations shows it migrating steadily northward, expanding the monsoon-influenced domain over northern China and shifting the peak precipitation belt with it. 该研究以降水与环流双重指标界定东亚夏季风北界，而非仅依赖降水。追踪 21 世纪模拟中的北界位置发现其持续北移，季风影响范围向华北扩展，降水峰值带随之移动。

Keywords: East Asian summer monsoon, northern boundary, climate projection, EC-Earth关键词：东亚夏季风、北界、气候预估、EC-Earth

Monsoon Dynamics and Variability季风动力学与变率

Changes in the north boundary of the East Asian summer monsoon in interglacials of last 800 ka

Wang Z., Zhang X.

Quaternary Sciences, 2020, 40(6): 1474–1485.

Proxy and geological evidence are compiled to reconstruct the monsoon northern boundary across successive interglacials of the last 800 ka. The boundary shifts are not uniform between interglacials, and their amplitude tracks the strength of insolation forcing rather than following a simple linear trend. 该研究综合代用资料与地质证据，重建了过去 80 万年各间冰期季风北界的位置。北界迁移在不同间冰期之间并不一致，其幅度随日射强迫强度变化，而非简单的线性趋势。

Keywords: interglacial, monsoon boundary, orbital forcing, Quaternary关键词：间冰期、季风北界、轨道强迫、第四纪

Vegetation–Climate Feedbacks植被—气候反馈

Differential vegetation feedback on global land monsoon during the Mid-Holocene and the Last Interglacial

Wang Z., Zhang Q., Chen J., Han Z.

Advances in Atmospheric Sciences, 2026, 43: 103–119.

Coupled climate–vegetation experiments are used to isolate the vegetation feedback on the global land monsoon in two contrasting interglacials. Vegetation feedback strengthens monsoon precipitation in both cases, but the magnitude differs substantially, indicating that the feedback is state-dependent rather than a fixed amplification. 研究利用气候—植被耦合试验，在两个对比鲜明的间冰期中分离植被对全球陆地季风的反馈。两次试验中植被反馈都增强了季风降水，但强度差异显著，说明该反馈依赖背景气候状态，而非固定的放大系数。

Keywords: vegetation feedback, global monsoon, Mid-Holocene, Last Interglacial, PMIP4关键词：植被反馈、全球季风、全新世中期、末次间冰期、PMIP4

Vegetation–Climate Feedbacks植被—气候反馈

Vegetation–climate feedbacks shaped early-to-mid Holocene precipitation and millet expansion on the Loess Plateau

Chen J.#, Zhang Q., Wang Z.#, Zhang Z., Ma M., Yang X., Chen F.

Science Advances, 2026 (in revising).

Model experiments are combined with proxy records to show that vegetation–climate feedbacks amplified early-to-mid Holocene precipitation over the Loess Plateau. The resulting wetter window coincides with the northward expansion of millet agriculture, linking a modelled feedback to the archaeological record. 研究将模式试验与代用记录结合，表明植被—气候反馈放大了全新世早中期黄土高原的降水。由此形成的湿润期与粟作农业的北向扩张在时间上吻合，把模式中的反馈机制与考古记录联系起来。

Keywords: Loess Plateau, Holocene, vegetation feedback, millet agriculture关键词：黄土高原、全新世、植被反馈、粟作农业

Paleoclimate Modelling and Model Development古气候模拟与模式开发

Modelling the late Pliocene climate with AWI-CM3 according to PlioMIP3 boundary conditions

Matos F., Stepanek C., Harig S., Streffing J., Zhang Q., Wang Z., Power K., Semmler T., Sidorenko D., Scholz P., Danilov S., Gravis P., Lohmann G.

Global and Planetary Change, 2025, 257: 105196.

A PlioMIP3-compliant AWI-CM3 configuration is documented and its simulated late Pliocene climate evaluated against proxy-based reconstructions. The study contributes a benchmark simulation to the third Pliocene Model Intercomparison Project and quantifies where the model reproduces and where it departs from the proxy record. 研究记录了符合 PlioMIP3 规范的 AWI-CM3 模式配置，并将其模拟的上新世晚期气候与代用资料重建结果进行对比。该工作为第三次上新世模式比较计划提供了基准模拟，并量化了模式再现与偏离代用记录的位置。

Keywords: PlioMIP3, AWI-CM3, late Pliocene, model intercomparison关键词：PlioMIP3、AWI-CM3、上新世晚期、模式比较

Paleoclimate Modelling and Model Development古气候模拟与模式开发

Miocene African topography induces decoupling of Somali Jet and South Asian summer monsoon rainfall

Han Z., Werner N., Wang Z., Li X., Yao Z., Zhang Q.

Nature Communications, 2025, 16: 7172.

Sensitivity experiments with progressively lowered African topography show that uplift reorganised low-level circulation during the Miocene, so that the Somali Jet and South Asian summer monsoon rainfall became decoupled. The result reframes topography, rather than ocean forcing alone, as a first-order control on monsoon geometry. 通过逐步降低非洲地形的敏感性试验，研究发现中新世的地形抬升重组了低层环流，使索马里急流与南亚季风降水发生解耦。这一结果说明地形而非仅海洋强迫，是控制季风空间结构的一级因子。

Keywords: African topography, Somali Jet, South Asian monsoon, Miocene关键词：非洲地形、索马里急流、南亚季风、中新世

Climate Variability and Teleconnections气候变率与遥相关

Quasi-stationary interhemispheric radiative forcing muted AMOC variability and enabled the mid-Holocene Green Sahara collapse

Zhang Q., Wang Z., Power K.

Nature Communications, 2026 (under review).

The study proposes that a quasi-stationary interhemispheric radiative forcing pattern suppressed Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation variability during the mid-Holocene. Reduced variability, rather than a monotonic weakening, created the conditions under which the Green Sahara vegetation collapsed. 研究提出，准定常的半球间辐射强迫型抑制了全新世中期大西洋经向翻转环流（AMOC）的变率。并非单调减弱，而是变率降低为绿色撒哈拉植被的崩溃创造了条件。

Keywords: AMOC, interhemispheric forcing, Green Sahara, Mid-Holocene关键词：AMOC、半球间强迫、绿色撒哈拉、全新世中期

Climate Variability and Teleconnections气候变率与遥相关

ENSO persistence prolongs winter-like teleconnections and reshapes boreal summer heatwaves across the Americas

Schultze A., Lu Z., Wang Z., Karami M. P., Zhang Q., Zheng M., Pugh T. A. M.

Nature Climate Change, 2026 (submitted).

Persistent ENSO conditions are shown to extend winter-like teleconnection patterns into boreal summer, reshaping both the frequency and the spatial pattern of heatwaves across the Americas. The finding links tropical Pacific persistence to mid-latitude summer extremes. 研究表明，持续的 ENSO 状态会将类冬季遥相关型延伸至北半球夏季，重塑美洲热浪的发生频率与空间格局。该发现把热带太平洋的持续性信号与中纬度夏季极端事件联系起来。

Keywords: ENSO, teleconnection, heatwaves, climate extremes关键词：ENSO、遥相关、热浪、极端气候

Ecosystem Water and Carbon Responses生态系统水碳响应

Weakening correlation and delaying response time of ecosystem water use efficiency to drought

Wang Z., Wu R., Liu Y., Zhang Z., Wen Z., Wang Z., Sitch S., Yuan W.

Hydrology and Earth System Sciences, 2026.

Global ecosystem water-use efficiency and drought indicators are analysed together to test how tightly the two remain coupled. The correlation weakens over recent decades while the lagged response lengthens, suggesting that ecosystems are buffering drought more slowly than before. 研究将全球生态系统水分利用效率与干旱指标联合分析，检验两者的耦合强度。近几十年二者相关性减弱而滞后响应时间延长，表明生态系统缓冲干旱的过程比以往更慢。

Keywords: water-use efficiency, drought, ecosystem response, global关键词：水分利用效率、干旱、生态系统响应、全球

Ecosystem Water and Carbon Responses生态系统水碳响应

Mechanisms behind evapotranspiration dynamics in the middle Yellow River Basin: Role of climate and vegetation

Wang Z., Ren H., Han S., Shi H., Wang Z., Liu Y., Wen Z.

Journal of Environmental Management, 2025, 394: 127318.

Evapotranspiration change in the middle Yellow River Basin is attributed to climate and vegetation drivers separately. Vegetation greening explains a substantial share of the increase, alongside a rising atmospheric demand for water driven by warming. 研究将黄河中游蒸散发变化分别归因于气候与植被驱动因子。植被绿化解释了增量的相当部分，同时增暖导致的大气需水量上升也起作用。

Keywords: evapotranspiration, vegetation greening, Yellow River Basin, attribution关键词：蒸散发、植被绿化、黄河流域、归因分析
